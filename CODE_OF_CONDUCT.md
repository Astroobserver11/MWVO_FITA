# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, and keep technical disagreement
technical. Harassment, personal attacks, and demeaning conduct are not
acceptable in issues, pull requests, or any other project space.

Reports may be sent to the maintainer at **astroobserver88@gmail.com**. Reports
will be handled confidentially.

Scientific criticism is welcome and expected — including of this project's own
claims. Several defects here were found by adversarial review, and a finding
that the maintainer overstated something is a success of the process, not an
attack on it.
