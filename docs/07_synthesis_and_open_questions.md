# Stage 7: Synthesis and Open Questions

## Unified Picture

The seven stages trace a single dynamical narrative:

1. **GCs are MSP factories** — 360 known pulsars, ~93% MSPs, 1,000× field efficiency per unit mass. Terzan 5 leads with 49. FAST GC FANS added 60 in 41 clusters; SKA projects ~1,700.

2. **Blue stragglers are the complementary tracer** — 3,000+ BSs across 48 GCs anti-correlate with density, collision rate, and dynamical age; correlate with binary fraction (r = 0.81). Dense cores are MSP-rich, BS-poor; lower-density regions are BS-rich, MSP-poor.

3. **VLBI astrometry anchors the frame** — PSR-pi/MSPSR-pi deliver microarcsecond parallaxes; SKA-VLBI with MultiView targets ~8 μas, reaching 12.5 kpc for half the sample. Independent distances and internal kinematics.

4. **ngVLA pathfinder hunts sMBHs** — 54 faint core sources (10–100 μJy) across 14 GCs; proper motions separate members from background AGN. Complements HST astrometric sMBH detections (oMEGACat BH-2).

5. **SKA-LOW adds steep-spectrum sensitivity** — low-frequency MSP census and core continuum; complements MID timing and VLBI.

6. **A coordinated five-program series** closes the loop: timing (A), VLBI parallax (B), ngVLA continuum (C), SKA-LOW survey (D), joint dynamical modeling (E).

7. **Open questions** remain at every interface.

## Open Questions

### Pulsar Population

- What is the true GC MSP luminosity function below FAST thresholds?
- Do core-collapsed clusters host IMBHs, and can pulsar accelerations detect them?
- What fraction of GC MSPs are isolated vs binary, and does the ratio trace encounter rate?

### Blue Stragglers

- Can N-body models reproduce the observed BS–binary correlation without invoking collisions?
- Do BS spatial distributions correlate with pulsar distributions at fixed encounter rate?
- Are there "blue straggler pulsars" — MSPs whose companions are BS-like evolved stars?

### Astrometry and Dynamics

- Can SKA-VLBI MultiView overcome Galactic-plane ionosphere for bulge GCs?
- What is the radio-loud fraction of GC sMBH candidates?
- Can joint HST + radio proper motions measure GC internal rotation?

### Multi-Wavelength Synergy

- How do GC pulsar DMs compare with HI and CO maps along the same lines of sight?
- Can SKA-LOW detect the thermal free-free continuum from GC cores?
- Do GC X-ray sources (Chandra) cross-match with radio core sources?
