# Stage 1: Globular Cluster Pulsar Population

## Overview

Globular clusters (GCs) host approximately 360 known radio pulsars across 46 clusters (as of early 2025), representing roughly 93% millisecond pulsars (MSPs). This constitutes nearly half the entire Galactic MSP census despite GCs containing under 1% of the Galaxy's stellar mass. Per unit mass, GCs are approximately 1,000 times more efficient at producing MSPs than the Galactic field.

## FAST GC FANS Survey

- Added 60 pulsars in 41 clusters as of early 2025
- 35 of the 60 are binaries
- Demonstrates that the known population is still a lower limit

## SKAO Projections (December 2025 White Paper)

- SKA-MID AA-star: more than double the known population
- SKA-MID AA-4: potentially ~1,700 pulsars across all Galactic GCs visible to SKA
- SKA-LOW: complementary low-frequency census, particularly for steep-spectrum sources

## Dynamical Context

- Single MSPs are ~40% of GC MSPs vs ~20% in the field
- Single MSPs concentrate in core-collapsed clusters
- N-body simulations suggest two formation channels: (1) tidal disruption of main-sequence stars by neutron stars, (2) collapse of heavy white-dwarf merger remnants
- The same dynamical encounters that recycle neutron stars into MSPs also shape the binary population feeding blue stragglers (Stage 2)
