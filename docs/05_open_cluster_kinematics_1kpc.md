# 05. Galactic Open Clusters Within 1 kpc: High-Resolution Kinematic Studies

**Date:** 2026-10-23
**Stage:** 5 of 7
**Question:** What high-resolution kinematic studies exist for galactic open clusters within 1 kpc?

---

## 5.1 Gaia-enabled expansion studies

### Kuhn et al. (Gaia DR2)
- 28 clusters aged 1-5 Myr
- >=75% are **expanding** at ~0.5 km/s (1D)
- 1D dispersions: 1-3 km/s
- Most are **supervirial**; some unbound
- **Key finding for dusty-plasma angle:** systems still embedded in molecular clouds are *less* likely to be expanding than partially or fully revealed ones — gas expulsion in real time

### Armstrong & Tan (2026)
- Gaia DR3 astrometry + calibrated radial velocities
- 23 young clusters <60 Myr within 1 kpc
- Most show **anisotropic expansion** even past 30 Myr
- Older clusters tend to expand **parallel to the Galactic plane** — natal cloud imprint survives long after gas is gone

### Harsha et al.
- 14 young open clusters within 1 kpc
- Transverse dispersions: **0.4-0.7 km/s**
- 5 clusters show **mass-dependent dispersion gradients** — a relaxation signature
- 5 clusters host possible **walkaway B and A stars**

### Tang (2026)
- Gaia DR3 radial velocities within 500 pc
- Median dispersion cut from 3.76 to **2.79 km/s** using color filtering
- Red-clump giants alone: dispersions **<1.6 km/s**

### APOGEE / GALAH
- High-resolution RVs for 100+ clusters
- Internal dispersions <2 km/s for clean samples
- Unresolved binaries inflate some measurements

## 5.2 The HST exception

The HST UV Legacy Survey (GO-13297, Piotto; Libralato 2022 kinematics release) included **exactly one open cluster** (IC 348) alongside 56 globulars. This demonstrates how rare space-based proper motions are for open clusters — Gaia already covers them well at <1 kpc.

## 5.3 The gap

**Nobody has done Anderson-style multi-epoch HST astrometry on a dense, dusty embedded cluster core at sub-milliarcsecond precision.** Gaia's resolution hits a wall in those environments (crowding + extinction + bright nebular background). This is a real opening for a proposal.
