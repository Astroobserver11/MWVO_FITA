# 06. Radio and Millimeter Tracers for Open Cluster Kinematics

**Date:** 2026-10-23
**Stage:** 6 of 7
**Question:** Are there open clusters with radial velocities established by millimeter astronomy (CO), radio continuum, or pulsars, that can be compared with Gaia kinematics?

## 6.1 Pulsar in/near open clusters

### PSR B1259-63 / LS 2883 + UBC 525 (THE flagship case)
- Gamma-ray binary: 47.7 ms pulsar, Be-star companion LS 2883 (~16.8 solar masses from Gaia)
- 2025 Gaia DR3 census: compelling membership evidence for open cluster **UBC 525** at 2.26 kpc
- VLBI parallax: 2.6 kpc; Gaia: 2.26 kpc (discrepancy ~340 pc)
- Systemic RV: ~0 km/s heliocentric (Miller-Jones H-alpha zero-point assumption)
- Space velocity: U=-72, V=-49, W=-3 km/s; peculiar 26+-8 km/s (LSR), 34+-13 km/s (Cen OB1)
- Drift over 300 kyr characteristic age: only ~8 pc
- **Modest kick, not a runaway** — strengthens birth-association case

### PSR J1922+3704 + NGC 6791
- Discovered by FAST in the direction of NGC 6791
- DM distance: 4.79 kpc; NGC 6791 Gaia distance: 4.19 kpc (overlap)
- If timing confirms membership: **first pulsar ever found in an open cluster**
- FAST follow-up planned: proper motion, HI absorption, deeper search

### 145-pulsar backward-orbit census
- 145 pulsars traced against ~3,000 open clusters with full kinematics
- 19 pulsars likely born in open clusters
- Collinder 132 probable birthplace of J0630-2834 (~1.6 Myr ago)
- **Main uncertainty:** pulsar RVs unknown; distributions assumed

## 6.2 Millimeter: YSO-molecular cloud associations

- 2026 paper: homogeneous 3D kinematics for 15 star-forming regions to 2.5 kpc (Gaia DR3 + spectroscopic RVs, 24,000+ young stars)
- YSO and open-cluster members agree to ~2 km/s per cloud -> treat as one tracer for bulk cloud motion
- Related catalog: 102 YSO-molecular cloud associations within 3.5 kpc with full 3D velocities
- Gas follows Galactic rotation + 8.6 km/s peculiar motion

## 6.3 The Orion Nebula Cluster: radio vs. optical tension

- **VLA (Dzib et al.):** radio proper motions -> **no expansion**
- **Gaia:** optical data -> expansion at outer edges
- **Tension unresolved** — the only system where radio and optical kinematics have been directly compared at this level
- ONC is at 414 pc (well within 1 kpc) -> Gaia most reliable here
- Discrepancy may be instrumental (reference frames, binary contamination) or physical (radio traces most embedded/youngest sources; Gaia traces optically revealed population)

## 6.4 The practical gap

**No open cluster yet has simultaneous pulsar timing, HI or CO radial velocities, and Gaia astrometry all reduced to one reference frame.** UBC 525 + B1259-63 is the nearest approach.
