# Stage 6: Proposed Observation Series — High-Resolution Radio Interferometry of GC Pulsars and Core Sources

## Motivation

The preceding stages establish: (1) GCs are the most efficient MSP factories in the Galaxy with ~360 known pulsars and a projected SKA yield of ~1,700; (2) blue stragglers anti-correlate with density and collision rate while correlating with binary fraction, implying encounter-driven complementarity between MSP and BS populations; (3) VLBI parallax campaigns (PSR-pi, MSPSR-pi) deliver microarcsecond astrometry but miss faint FAST sources; (4) an ngVLA pathfinder targets 54 faint core sources (10–100 μJy) across 14 GCs as sMBH candidates; (5) SKA-LOW offers steep-spectrum sensitivity and wide-field continuum capability.

A coordinated multi-facility program can close the loop: identify, time, and astrometrically characterize GC pulsars and core radio sources in a common reference frame.

## Proposed Series

### Program A: SKA-MID Timing + Astrometry of FAST GC Pulsars

- **Targets**: FAST GC FANS discoveries not yet timed at high precision
- **Frequencies**: 1.4 GHz (discovery band), 650 MHz and 3 GHz for timing noise characterization
- **Cadence**: monthly for 2 years minimum for phase connection; quarterly thereafter
- **Deliverables**: timing solutions, period derivatives, binary orbital parameters, DM measurements
- **Priority clusters**: Terzan 5 (49 pulsars, highest density), 47 Tuc (25), M15 (14, core-collapsed)

### Program B: SKA-VLBI Parallax of GC MSPs

- **Targets**: brightest GC MSPs with existing timing solutions (subset of Program A)
- **Frequencies**: 1.6 GHz primary; MultiView calibration with ≥3 calibrators
- **Baseline**: 2–3 year campaign for sub-10 microarcsecond parallax
- **Deliverables**: independent GC distances, systemic proper motions, internal velocity dispersions from multi-pulsar samples
- **Science**: GC distance ladder, IMBH mass constraints from pulsar acceleration gradients

### Program C: ngVLA-Long + VLBA Continuum of GC Cores

- **Targets**: 54 faint core sources (10–100 μJy at 5 GHz) across 14 GCs per the ngVLA memo
- **Frequencies**: 5 GHz primary; 1.4 GHz and 15 GHz for spectral indices
- **Deliverables**: proper motions distinguishing cluster members from background AGN; radio spectral energy distributions
- **Science**: sMBH candidate confirmation, radio-loud MSP census, core radio source luminosity function

### Program D: SKA-LOW Steep-Spectrum GC Survey

- **Targets**: all GCs visible to SKA-LOW with |b| < 20° (bulge concentration)
- **Frequencies**: 110–350 MHz
- **Deliverables**: steep-spectrum MSP candidates, low-frequency continuum map of GC cores
- **Science**: faint MSP population, scattering-limited source detection, cross-match with FAST and SKA-MID catalogs

### Program E: Joint Dynamical Modeling

- **Inputs**: Programs A–D astrometry + existing HST proper motions (HSTPROMO, Anderson et al.) + Gaia for outer regions
- **Method**: multi-mass Jeans modeling and/or N-body comparison with observed pulsar and BS spatial distributions
- **Deliverables**: GC mass profiles, IMBH upper limits, encounter-rate maps correlated with BS and MSP distributions
- **Science**: unified dynamical picture connecting Stages 1–2

## Resource Requirements

| Program | Facility Time | Duration |
|---------|---------------|----------|
| A | SKA-MID ~200 hr/yr | 3 yr |
| B | SKA-VLBI ~100 hr/yr | 3 yr |
| C | ngVLA-Long/VLBA ~150 hr/yr | 2 yr |
| D | SKA-LOW ~100 hr | 1 yr |
| E | Compute (N-body) | Ongoing |

## Risk Factors

- FAST GC pulsar faintness may limit VLBI (Program B) to a bright subset
- Ionospheric calibration near the Galactic plane remains challenging for SKA-VLBI
- ngVLA pathfinder construction timeline (before decade's end) may delay Program C
- SKA-LOW GC core scattering could limit low-frequency detection for bulge clusters
